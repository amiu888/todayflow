# Contacts: add people directly to a task

![Task detail with a linked contact](images/task-contact.png)

## Link an existing contact

1. Click a task to open its detail window.
2. Find the **Contacts** section under the status/priority pills.
3. Click **Add Contact**.
4. Search the popover, then click a person. The contact is linked immediately.

## Create a new contact without leaving the task

1. Open **Add Contact**.
2. At the bottom of the popover, type a **Name**, **Email**, or **Phone**.
3. Click **Create and Add**. TodayFlow creates the contact and links it to this task in one step.
4. If the email or phone matches an existing contact, TodayFlow links that person instead of creating a duplicate.

## Remove a contact

Click the **×** next to a linked contact. The contact stays in the Contacts directory; only the task link is removed.

## Groups and drag/drop

Open **Contacts** from the person icon in the Today header (or **View → Contacts**).

- Create top-level groups with **New Group**.
- Right-click a group → **New Subgroup** to add a second level.
- Drag contacts from **All Contacts** onto a group to add them.
- Drag a contact from one group to another to move it.
- Drag contact cards from macOS Contacts or Outlook directly onto a group to import and add them in one step.
- **Duplicates** finds contacts sharing an email or phone and offers merge options.
