# TodayFlow privacy policy

*Last updated: November 27, 2026*

TodayFlow (TodayFlow Tasks on iPhone, and TodayFlow for Mac) does not collect your data. There are no accounts with us, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## Where your tasks live

- **On your device.** Your tasks, notes, checklists, trays, contacts you add and attachments are stored in the app on your iPhone or Mac.
- **On your own server, only if you set up sync.** If you pair the app with a TodayFlow sync server, your data goes to that server, which **you run** (for example on your own NAS). We don't run it, and we can't see it. Unpair any time in Settings ▸ Sync.

## Screenshots, messages and AI

- When you paste or share a screenshot, the app reads its text **on your device** (Apple's Vision framework).
- Titles, summaries and to-do suggestions come from **Apple Intelligence on your device**, when your device has it. Nothing is sent to us or to any other company.
- On the Mac you can choose to connect your own AI service (an OpenAI-compatible address you enter). Text is then sent only to the service you chose, under its own privacy policy. This is off unless you turn it on.

## Permissions

- **Notifications:** to remind you before tasks are due. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
- **Local network:** to reach your own sync server on your home network.

## Children

TodayFlow is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/amiu888/todayflow/issues.
