# TodayFlow support

**Get help or report a problem:** open an issue at https://github.com/amiu888/todayflow/issues. Say whether you use the iPhone or the Mac app and its version (Settings ▸ About).

## Quick answers

- **Add a task from a screenshot or message:** copy it, then New Task ▸ Paste. Or share it to TodayFlow from Photos, Messages or Safari.
- **Find the to-dos in a message:** open the task ▸ Checklist ▸ Find To-dos.
- **Sync your iPhone and Mac:** run your own TodayFlow server (see the server guide in this repository), then Settings ▸ Sync on each device with the same user's pairing code.
- **Sync stopped:** Settings ▸ Sync shows the reason. "Signed out" means pair again with your code; your tasks stay on the device.
